so_what_en: "This is not another generator; it addresses what happens after generation. Anyone doing interiors knows the render is instant but the model does not check whether a wardrobe clears the doorway, suits the occupants or uses the daylight. The useful move is treating your specification set as an asset: whoever can change it once and have every schedule follow stops burning nights reconciling lists."
